Can a model tell good pairs from bad ones?

How to read this

Sharpe ratio is return per unit of risk. Above 1 is good, near 0 means no edge. Drawdown is the biggest fall from a previous peak. Cost per side is what each buy or sell costs in basis points (1 bp = 0.01%), covering commissions and slippage. The strategy trades often, so small costs add up quickly.

The test is walk-forward: pairs are picked on one year of past prices, traded on the next quarter, then re-picked. The pair-quality model is trained only on earlier windows and scored on later ones. An AUC of 0.50 is a coin flip.
